Considering the social needs of our group project, the approaches me and my community partners employed to resolve the need for students to be able to make their own honor society projects seem to be very effective. The reason I believe so is because our solution is unique to a problem, as there is not much websites to help with community projects from high school students. In working with our client associated with the Arkansas Civic Action Network, this solution can be spread amongst the state. In the making of this project, being able to take initiative is a most valuable skill in carrying out the social change work we were doing together. I got the website page started and made the database, which helped my peers in their work. It works well with the limitations I have of not having much experience in styling or not being able to get all the work done, because being able to convey what you want clearly among the group that has experience in doing so by starting off strong can help build up projects in places where you can't.
